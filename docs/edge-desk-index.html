Rules this screen applies

Wheel

  • Stock under ~$60 so one contract fits a $10k book
  • Put strike near 0.20 delta, 20–45 days out
  • No earnings before expiry. Earnings inside the window moves a name to the watch list
  • Bid/ask spread under 25% of mid, open interest over 500
  • Rank = annualised yield, adjusted for spread, IV percentile and IV vs realised vol
  • Max 30% of the wheel sleeve per name (or one contract, if larger)

Option plays

  • Buy spreads where IV is below realised vol (cheap options)
  • Sell spreads where IV is well above realised vol (rich options)
  • Defined risk only. Max loss per trade ≤ the plays sleeve
  • Take profits at 50% of max; exit debit spreads at −50%

Stocks

  • Above its price 52 weeks ago (uptrend)
  • Within 15% of its 52-week high
  • 52-week IV percentile under 50% (not in a vol spike)
  • Average daily turnover over $500M

Wheel management

  • Buy back puts at 50% profit and resell
  • Roll down and out for a credit if tested with more than 7 days left
  • If assigned, sell calls at or above your net cost basis
  • Skip new entries the week of a name's earnings
